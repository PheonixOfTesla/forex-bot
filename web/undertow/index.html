How Molua works

One player is secretly given the notebook. Whoever holds it can eliminate you simply by writing your name in it.

Everyone else is the village, and the village has one job: find who has the notebook — without eliminating their own people.

  1. Nightfall. Everyone waits. Whoever has the notebook writes one name in it, and an order for them to obey.
  2. Forty Seconds. The chosen player performs that order out loud, in the room, before they go. Watch how they do it.
  3. The Gathering. Argue. Accuse. Defend yourself.
  4. The Verdict. Most votes is out. Then it starts again.

The village wins by finding the notebook. The notebook wins by outlasting the village.
